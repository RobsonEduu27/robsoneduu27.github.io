Channel Dashboard is a private, read-only tool used by a single YouTube creator to read the statistics of the creator's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Reads public channel and video data through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads the creator's own channel analytics (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and estimated revenue) through the YouTube Analytics API, only after the channel owner signs in and grants read-only access.
  • Shows the numbers in local reports on the creator's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It never uploads, edits or deletes videos, comments or channel settings.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
